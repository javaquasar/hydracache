New here? Three things to know

  1. The circles are cluster nodes. ★ marks the leader. Click a node for its details and actions; click a line to pick a link.
  2. Step advances one deterministic step; Play keeps going. Values that changed on the last step flash, and are listed under the graph.
  3. The verdict is the live invariant checker. If it turns red, the run pauses on the first violating step and shows how to reproduce it.
