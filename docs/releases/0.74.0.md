## Activation and rollback

Upgrading a server requires replacing the binary and restarting its service
through the normal deployment procedure. The exact RC passed the recorded
same-volume and three-node upgrade and rollback checks against 0.73.
The stable binary requires its own compatible-reader and rollback result.
W11 tooling is provisioned independently and uses its original lifecycle
and process-identity checks.
